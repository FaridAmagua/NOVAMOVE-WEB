Ibiza is often reduced to its summer nightlife. Those who actually live here year-round know a completely different island: calm, family-oriented, beautifully protected. Here are the five reasons families choose it for relocation.

## 1. Safety

Ibiza has one of the lowest crime rates in Europe. Children walk to school, families ride bikes between villages, and the sense of community is genuine.

## 2. Nature

UNESCO Biosphere Reserve status since 2019. Protected coastline, clean water, organic farms, and protected agricultural land. Living next to nature — without sacrificing connectivity.

## 3. Privacy

Unlike Marbella or Mallorca, Ibiza remains genuinely discreet. Many estates sit on private hillsides, far from view. High-profile families value that.

## 4. International schools

Morna International College and the Montessori schools serve families who want continuity with international curricula in English, Spanish and German.

## 5. Year-round lifestyle

Winter averages 16°C, traffic disappears, restaurants re-open with locals, and you finally get to know the island. Many newcomers say this is when they fell in love with Ibiza.
