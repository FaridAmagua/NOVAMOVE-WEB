Portugal continues to be a magnet for European families and investors. We compare Lisbon and Porto across the dimensions that matter most for relocation decisions.

## Lifestyle

**Lisbon** — Cosmopolitan, hills, ocean, world-class gastronomy. The pace picks up in the centre; quieter in Estrela, Lapa, Cascais.

**Porto** — More intimate, walkable, raw charm. Strong food scene, port wine heritage, and a much lower cost of living.

## Real estate (avg. €/m², Q2 2026)

| District | Lisbon | Porto |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prime city centre | 9,500 – 13,000 | 5,500 – 7,800 |
| Premium suburban | 5,800 – 7,500 | 3,800 – 5,000 |
| Beach (Cascais / Vila Nova de Gaia) | 6,500 – 9,000 | 4,200 – 5,800 |

## Tax regime

The NHR 2.0 (IFICI) regime grants a flat 20% tax rate for 10 years on qualifying foreign-source income for new residents. Both cities are equally covered.

## Verdict

- **For lifestyle + connectivity**: Lisbon wins.
- **For yield + lower entry**: Porto wins (gross yield 5–7% vs 3.5–4.5% in Lisbon prime).
- **For families**: Cascais (near Lisbon) and Boavista (Porto) are top picks.
