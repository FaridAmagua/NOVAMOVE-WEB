Marbella remains one of the favourite destinations for families and professionals seeking quality of life in the Mediterranean. This guide covers the essentials we handle in every onboarding.

## 1. Documentation and residency

Before moving, define your residency strategy:

- **EU citizen**: empadronamiento (local registration) and green NIE. No visa required, Social Security enrolment.
- **Non-EU**: Golden Visa (real estate investment from €500,000) or Non-Lucrative Visa.
- **Long-term**: EU Long-Term Residence Card after 5 years of continuous legal residence.

## 3. Tax for residents

The Special Impatriates Regime ("Beckham Law") allows a flat 24% tax rate on the first €600,000 for six years if you move from outside Spain. It's one of the major competitive advantages of relocating to Spain.

## 4. International education

International families usually pick between:

- **Public school + language support** — most integrated option.
- **Concerted bilingual school** — good balance.
- **Private international school** — British School, Aloha College, Swans.

## 5. Realistic cost of living

For a family of four in Marbella (2026):

- Premium rental: €3,500 – €5,500
- International school (2 kids): €2,500 – €4,500
- Private insurance + utilities: €800 – €1,200
- Lifestyle and leisure: €1,500 – €3,000
