The Beckham Law (Special Impatriates Regime) is one of the most powerful tax tools for those moving to Spain. Here's what changes in 2026 and how to leverage it.

## What is it?

It lets you pay a flat **24% tax rate on the first €600,000** and 47% above, for **6 years**, instead of Spain's progressive IRPF that hits 47% from €300,000 upwards.

## Main requirements

1. You have not been a Spanish tax resident for the previous 5 years.
2. You move your tax residence to Spain.
3. You are hired by a Spanish company, or come to manage one from abroad, or are self-employed with genuine economic activity in Spain.
4. You opt in within **6 months of registering with Social Security**.

## 2026 changes

- The €600,000 cap remains, but dividends and capital gains continue to be taxed at the standard progressive rate.
- The regime expands to include international remote workers of non-resident companies.
- Income from foreign accounts must be reported in full, even though taxed at 24%.

## Common mistakes

- **Applying outside the deadline** — 6 months is strict.
- **Not proving genuine economic activity** — tax authorities require invoices, clients, contracts.
- **Assuming it covers passive income** — dividends, rental income and capital gains are excluded.
